## Why teams use it

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="No migration" icon="approve-check">
		Keyspaces stays your source of truth and your application keeps writing to it unchanged. Turn on the
		table's change stream and the index follows.
	</Card>
	<Card title="The index stays right" icon="random">
		Inserts, updates, deletes and expiries all reach the index. A failed batch is retried, never skipped, and a
		restart resumes where it stopped. Tested against live AWS.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Your data stays in your account" icon="setting">
		Runs on your ECS under an IAM role that can read one table and write one index. With a self-hosted model,
		your text never leaves your VPC.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Pick your model" icon="puzzle">
		Any open Sentence-Transformers model on Hugging Face TEI, Amazon Bedrock (Titan, Cohere), or an
		OpenAI-compatible API.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Pays only for real changes" icon="star">
		A row is embedded only when its text changes. Updates to other columns cost nothing, and metadata changes
		reuse the stored vector.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Open source" icon="open-book">
		Apache-2.0. Read every line before it touches your data, and change it if you need to.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
